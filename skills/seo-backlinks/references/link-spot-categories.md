# Link Spot Categories: The Ninja Linking Catalog

**Spot quality changes over time. Verify each spot before use.** Platforms change link attributes, get deindexed, die, or drown in spam. Before working any spot below, run the four-point check from the main skill: indexed (site: search), moderated, follow or nofollow on a live example, real traffic. This catalog is a starting point for hand-made acquisition, not a list to blast. One account, one honest profile, real contributions.

Link attribute notes ("nofollow typical", "varies") reflect commonly observed defaults; platforms change policy without notice, so confirm on a published example.

## Family 1: Universal spots (any industry)

**What you gain.** Foundation link volume, control of the first page of branded search results, entity confirmation for AI models (consistent profiles teach LLMs who the brand is), and a long tail of citation surfaces. Links are mostly nofollow, which is exactly what a natural foundation looks like. Traffic per spot is modest but nonzero; the aggregate is meaningful.

**How to contribute cleanly.** Complete every profile fully (logo, description, links, category), reuse the same positioning sentence everywhere, keep profiles alive (an abandoned 2019 profile signals neglect). On publishing platforms, post genuinely useful content, never spun filler. Add the profiles to your Organization schema sameAs array (see seo-schema-markup).

## Family 2: Directories and local citations

**What you gain.** Trust and entity confirmation, local pack eligibility for physical businesses, and consistent NAP data that AI assistants use to answer "near me" and "is X legit" queries. Links are mixed follow/nofollow; the citation consistency matters more than the link attribute.

**How to contribute cleanly.** Identical NAP (name, address, phone) everywhere, real categories, no keyword stuffing in the business name. **All local citation strategy, priority lists, and NAP audits belong to the seo-local skill; this family is listed here only so the ninja inventory is complete.**

## Family 3: Topical forums (the relevance goldmine)

**What you gain.** The most topically relevant links available for free, often dofollow inside threads, plus real referral traffic from people actively asking about your subject, plus threads that AI models read as authentic consensus. This family has the highest payoff and the highest barrier: forums require reputation before tolerating links.

**How to contribute cleanly.** Strict 95/5 rule: 95% genuine answers, 5% links, and only where the link answers the question better than text alone could. Build the account first (complete profile, 15-20 helpful posts over several weeks), then link rarely. Forum signature links are mostly worthless and often flagged; in-thread contextual links are the prize. One banned account costs every link it ever placed.

## Family 4: Communities, SaaS directories, and review platforms

**What you gain.** The heaviest AI citation surfaces in existence. Review platforms dominate "best X" and "X alternatives" answers; Reddit and community threads feed model consensus (with extreme volatility, see the main skill's GEO layer); SaaS directories rank for category and alternative queries. Links are mostly nofollow; the mention value outweighs the link value here.

**How to contribute cleanly.** Communities: 95/5 rule, participate as a named human, disclose affiliation when discussing your own product (most subreddits and groups require it, and undisclosed self-promotion gets banned and screenshotted). Directories: complete listings with real screenshots and accurate category placement. Review platforms: ask genuine customers for reviews; never fabricate or incentivize dishonest reviews (platforms detect it, and it is illegal in most markets).

## Working the catalog

1. Filter each family for the niche and market language (link from sites in the language of the market you target).
2. Run the four-point verification on every spot before investing time.
3. Schedule spots into the 90-day progression: Family 1 and 2 first (no reputation needed), Families 3 and 4 from week one as account building, links from month two.
4. Log every placement in the tracking table (date, spot, URL placed, target page, anchor, follow/nofollow, status).
5. Re-verify quarterly: dead spots out, new spots in from the footprints.
