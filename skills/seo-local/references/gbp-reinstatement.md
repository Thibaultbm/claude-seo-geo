# Google Business Profile Suspension: Reinstatement Procedure

Follow in order. Do not skip the compliance pass: appealing a profile that still violates guidelines wastes the appeal.

## 0. Do not do this

- Do not create a new profile for the same business. Duplicates violate Google's guidelines, make reinstatement harder, and can get both listings removed.
- Do not submit an appeal before fixing the profile.
- Do not submit multiple thin appeals in a row. Attempts are limited in practice; one complete appeal beats five rushed ones.
- Do not edit the profile repeatedly while an appeal is pending.

## 1. Identify the suspension type and likely trigger

| Type | Symptom | Common triggers |
|---|---|---|
| Soft suspension | Profile still visible, owner loses management access | Ownership or access anomalies |
| Hard suspension | Profile removed from Maps and Search | Guideline violations, re-verification failures |

Most frequent triggers seen in the field (field heuristic from 115+ agency audits):

- Keywords or city added to the business name
- Address change, category change, or many edits in a short window
- Virtual office, coworking, or PO box address presented as a storefront
- Service-area business showing a home address instead of hiding it
- New manager or owner added from an unusual account
- Industry under stricter scrutiny (locksmiths, garage doors, lawyers, plumbers, rehab)

## 2. Bring the profile to full compliance

- Business name = exact real-world name, no trade or city additions
- One profile per real location; service-area business hides the address and lists areas
- Categories match the actual business
- Address is a real, staffed location (or hidden for service-area setups)
- Phone and website live and matching the location page
- Hours accurate

## 3. Gather the evidence pack

Prepare all of it before opening the appeal form:

- [ ] Business registration document (company registry extract, trade license)
- [ ] Proof of address in the business name: utility bill, lease, or tax notice, recent
- [ ] Photos of the storefront with permanent signage visible, plus interior
- [ ] Photo of a business vehicle with branding, if service-area
- [ ] Website URL showing the same name, address, phone
- [ ] Any license required by the trade (legal, medical, locksmith)

Documents must show the exact same business name and address as the profile. Mismatches are the most common reason appeals fail (field heuristic).

## 4. Submit the appeal

1. Use Google's appeals tool (support.google.com/business/answer/12475845) with the account that manages the profile.
2. State factually: what the business is, where it operates, what was fixed, and attach the full evidence pack when the form allows uploads.
3. One appeal, complete. Note the case reference.

## 5. While waiting

- Typical resolution ranges from a few days to several weeks; do not refresh-edit the profile.
- Keep serving customers through the website's location page; it keeps ranking organically while the profile is down (one more reason every location needs its own page).
- If the appeal is denied and evidence was incomplete, the follow-up contact must add new evidence, not repeat the same.

## 6. After reinstatement

- Freeze risky edits (name, address, primary category) for several weeks.
- Re-check reviews and photos survived.
- Document the trigger and the fix so the next manager does not reintroduce it.
