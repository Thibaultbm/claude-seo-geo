# Homepage and offer page that rank, get cited and sell

The homepage is the strongest page of a small site: most backlinks land there, every branded search lands there, and AI assistants answering "who is {brand}" or "best {category} coach" read it first. The flagship offer page is where the money is made. On expert and coaching sites both are usually a stack of slogans, three icons, a video and a generic FAQ ("What is poker?"): 800 words, no bold, no numbers, no structured data. That page cannot rank on the category term, gives an AI engine nothing to quote and gives a buyer no reason to trust.

This skill fixes that in four phases (spec, audit, benchmark, build, see `docs/page-skill-standard.md`) with a measurable finish line.

## Company knowledge first (Obsidian)

If the working environment contains an Obsidian vault or any local knowledge base (a folder of .md notes, often with a .obsidian directory), read the relevant notes before writing: offer facts, real prices, real client results, founder story and credentials, positioning, competitors, social profile URLs, and the SEO action log. Every block of a homepage asserts facts about the business, so invented numbers are the main failure mode. At the end of the session, append what was published to the vault's SEO action log. Vault structure and protocols: the obsidian-brain skill.

## When to use

- A homepage or sales page is thin, slogan-only, or has only a generic FAQ.
- An audit (`seo_audit.py`) reports `no_bold`, `thin_content`, `schema_none`, `schema_entity`, `home_no_money_links`, `social_placeholder` or a low placement coverage on the homepage.
- A site is being built from scratch, or generated by an agent, and the block list must be decided first.
- The owner asks "what are my competitors doing that I am not".

## Phase 1. The spec

### 1.1 Facts the spec needs

Ask for, or pull from the vault, before writing a line. Missing facts become visible placeholders `{to confirm}`, never inventions.

| Fact | Used in |
|---|---|
| Category term and its search variants (what buyers type: "coaching poker", "formation poker", "agence SEO Lyon") | Title, H1, first 100 words, H2s (HOME-20) |
| The outcome promised, with a number and a timeframe | Hero sub-line, results block (HOME-02, HOME-07) |
| Proof numbers: clients served, years, results, ratings with their platform and count | Proof strip, results, founder block (HOME-03, HOME-22, HOME-25) |
| 3-6 client results with first name, starting point, result, timeframe; video testimonials | Results and testimonials (HOME-07, HOME-08) |
| Founder credentials that can be checked (years, track record, press, certifications, public profiles) | Founder block, Person schema (HOME-09, HOME-26) |
| The offer: format, contents, time commitment, price or price range, guarantee, process to join | Offer cards, sales page (HOME-06, OFFER-05 to OFFER-14) |
| Real social profile URLs (LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, X, TikTok, community) | Footer, founder block, `sameAs` (HOME-27) |
| The 8-12 objections prospects actually raise (sales calls, emails, DMs, comments) | FAQ (HOME-15, OFFER-16) |

### 1.2 Homepage requirements (HOME)

Common requirements C-01 to C-27: skills/seo-geo-audit/references/common-page-spec.md. They apply to all three pages and are not repeated here. Thresholds are field heuristics from 115+ agency audits unless marked measured; "14 sites" counts come from the October 2026 coaching benchmark in `references/block-library.md` section 5. Block details (minimum spec per block) live in `references/block-library.md` section 1, same numbering.

| ID | Requirement | Threshold | Verified by | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HOME-01 | Header | Logo, 4-6 menu items (offers, results, about, blog, contact), one CTA button in a contrasting color, sticky | section_audit `cta`; manual | The CTA stays visible at every scroll position |
| HOME-02 | Hero | H1 = **category term** + outcome, never a slogan; sub-line with one proof number; primary CTA (main conversion) and secondary CTA (low commitment: free training, guide, quiz); real founder or product image, eager-loaded | h1_missing, kw_not_in_intro, lcp_lazy; manual for the outcome | The first screen answers what, for whom, why trust, what next |
| HOME-03 | Proof strip | Rating with platform and count, client count, years, press or partner logos | section_audit `rating_summary`, `social_proof_logos`, `trust_badges` | Borrowed authority before any claim |
| HOME-04 | Problem | 3-6 pains in the buyer's own words (from calls, comments, reviews), each with what changes, at least one with a number | manual | Recognition keeps the right reader scrolling |
| HOME-05 | Named method | A name and 3-5 pillars, each with one line and one number | section_audit `process_block`; manual | What competitors cannot copy and what AI engines quote as an entity |
| HOME-06 | Offer routing | One card per offer: who it is for, format, price or "from", body link to its page | home_no_money_links; section_audit `internal_links`, `price_in_text` | Routes the homepage authority to the money pages |
| HOME-07 | Results with numbers | 3-6 results: first name, starting point, result, timeframe; screenshots or graphs; links to full case studies | no_stats; manual | Specific outcomes are the strongest conversion and citation material; numbers-based proof is strong on only 4 of 14 sites (measured) |
| HOME-08 | Testimonials | Video (YouTube embed with a text summary under each) plus written ones with real first name, photo, situation | section_audit `reviews`, `video` | Video proves the person exists; the summary makes it readable by crawlers |
| HOME-09 | Founder block | Real photo, 80-150 words, credentials with numbers, link to the about page, real social profiles | section_audit `author_block`; manual | E-E-A-T: who stands behind the promise |
| HOME-10 | Comparison table | This offer versus the usual alternatives (free content, other programs, doing it alone), 5-8 criteria, an HTML table | section_audit `comparison_table`; home_no_structure | Tables are the most extracted format in AI answers and the most direct answer to "why you"; 8 of 14 sites compare (measured) |
| HOME-11 | For / not for | Two short lists | home_no_structure; manual | Qualification raises conversion and lowers refunds |
| HOME-12 | Guarantee | Exact terms (duration, condition), only when one exists | section_audit `guarantee` | Risk reversal; vague guarantees read as traps; 3 of 14 sites, all English (measured) |
| HOME-13 | Lead magnet | Free training, guide, checklist, quiz or tool with an email form | section_audit `form`; manual | Captures the 95% not ready to buy today (field heuristic); 11 of 14 sites have one (measured) |
| HOME-14 | Latest articles | 3 cards with cover image, title, excerpt, date | section_audit `freshness_signal`; manual | Shows the site is alive and links the blog from the strongest page |
| HOME-15 | Objection FAQ | 8-12 real objections (price, time, level, difference from free content, results timeline, refund, support, how to join), 2-4 sentence answers, `<details>` with the text in the HTML; no definitions | section_audit `faq`; manual for the questions | Objections answered in writing replace a sales call; question-answer pairs are extracted by AI engines |
| HOME-16 | Descriptive block | 300+ words of prose on the category term, H2 phrased as the question buyers ask, bold on key facts | thin_content; manual | Gives retrieval and ranking real text when the rest of the page is short blocks |
| HOME-17 | Final CTA | Promise, one proof, one button | section_audit `cta` | The last conversion surface |
| HOME-18 | Footer | Real social profiles, contact, legal pages, disclaimers, sitemap-level links | social_placeholder, social_missing; section_audit `contact_details` | Entity consistency and trust; never placeholder icons |
| HOME-19 | Length | 1500-1800 words of real text, never under the competitor median (C-08 floor for this page type) | thin_content; page_benchmark.py `words` | A page of headlines gives ranking and retrieval nothing to work with |
| HOME-20 | Category term is the target keyword of C-01, C-03 and C-10 | Title "{what the business does} for {audience} \| {brand}", never "{Brand} \| Home"; term in title, H1, first 100 words, 2+ H2s, meta description, one image alt; placement 60%+ | kw_placement_low, kw_not_in_intro | The H1 is the second strongest relevance signal after the title; the homepage title is the highest-authority title on the site |
| HOME-21 | Semantic bolding (C-09 with the category term) | Category term bold once in the first paragraph; then one bold phrase every 100-150 words on a benefit, a number or a key fact; never whole sentences, never decoration | no_bold, kw_not_bold | Cheapest visible relevance and scannability signal; skimmers read the bold and the H2s only |
| HOME-22 | Numbers in every block | Each block carries at least one figure: results, clients, years, hours, price, timeframe | no_stats; citability `statistics`; manual per block | Statistics raise generative visibility (Princeton GEO study, about +32%, measured); specific claims convert better than adjectives |
| HOME-23 | Proof attribution | Each number says where it comes from: client name, platform, date, screenshot | manual (extends C-14) | Unattributed numbers read as marketing to buyers and quality systems |
| HOME-24 | Visible price | Price, range or "from" on the offer cards and the offer page, with instalments or an annual option when they exist; if the price is given only after a call, say so and why | section_audit `price_in_text`, `pricing_block` | 12 of 14 coaching sites show a price, 5 show payment options (measured); hiding it sends buyers and assistants to the ones that do |
| HOME-25 | Dated ratings | Rating + platform + review count + date read ("4.5/5 Trustpilot, 1253 avis, relevé le 9 août 2026") | section_audit `rating_summary`; manual for the date | A dated, counted rating is checkable, a bare star row is not; only 5 of 14 sites do it (measured) |
| HOME-26 | Checkable credentials | Founder linked to third-party profiles that confirm the claims (the field's public results databases, press, Wikipedia or Wikidata when they exist); the same URLs in Person `sameAs` | manual; schema_fields | Claims an assistant can cross-check become entity facts; claims it cannot are ignored; 4 of 14 sites do it (measured) |
| HOME-27 | Real social profiles on both entities | C-20 applied to the footer and the founder block; Organization `sameAs` and Person `sameAs` list the same real URLs | social_placeholder, social_missing, schema_fields | Entity consistency between the site, the profiles and the graph |
| HOME-28 | Honest claims (C-24 applied) | Results stated as observed outcomes of named clients, never as a guarantee; "n°1", "le meilleur", "leader" only with a cited source next to them; countdowns and limited places only when real | claim_no_disclaimer, superlative_claim; manual for urgency | Unbacked superlatives lower trust; fake urgency is a deceptive commercial practice under EU consumer law |
| HOME-29 | Regulated-topic notices | Income or results disclaimer next to the claim and in the footer; gambling: 18+, responsible-gambling mention, national helpline in the site-wide footer, a responsible-play page; editorial transparency pages linked from the footer | claim_no_disclaimer, gambling_notice_missing; manual | Your Money or Your Life topics; ARPP and FTC rules on testimonials with results; 7 of 14 poker coaching sites show the notice (measured). Detail: "Regulated and money topics" below |
| HOME-30 | Body links | Every offer page, the about page and 2-3 pillar articles linked from the body with descriptive anchors, not only the menu (C-16 applied) | home_no_money_links, generic_anchor; section_audit `internal_links` | The homepage's authority flows only through body links with descriptive anchors |
| HOME-31 | Homepage schema | Organization (name, url, logo, `sameAs` to every real profile, founder) + WebSite + the founder as Person with `sameAs`, one `@graph`; nothing marked up that the page does not show | schema_none, schema_entity, schema_fields, jsonld_invalid | Entity graph for AI engines; 11 of 14 sites ship a JSON-LD graph (measured), the client without it is in the bottom 3 |
| HOME-32 | Contact reachable | Contact details or a contact route reachable from the homepage | section_audit `contact_details` | A buyer ready to act must not hunt |
| HOME-33 | Shared competitor blocks | Every block present on 3+ benchmarked competitors (page_benchmark.py: half or more) is present | page_benchmark.py block gaps | The SERP shows which page shape Google already rewards; that list is the minimum, not the ceiling |

### 1.3 Flagship offer page requirements (OFFER)

The sales sequence, in this order. HOME-20 to HOME-30 apply to this page too (category or offer name as keyword, bolding, numbers, attribution, price, ratings, credentials, profiles, honest claims, notices, body links). Block details: `references/block-library.md` section 2, same numbering.

| ID | Requirement | Threshold | Verified by | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OFFER-01 | Hero | H1 = offer name + outcome; sub-line with the promise and timeframe; video sales letter or founder video (YouTube embed with chapters or a text summary); primary CTA | h1_missing; section_audit `video`, `cta` | The first screen sells the outcome, not the format |
| OFFER-02 | Proof strip | Rating, client count, result headline | section_audit `rating_summary`, `social_proof_logos` | Trust before the pitch |
| OFFER-03 | Problem and the cost of staying put | Buyer's words, with numbers | manual | Makes the price read as smaller than the cost of inaction |
| OFFER-04 | The method | Named, 3-5 pillars, each linked to what the client does with it | section_audit `process_block`; manual | The method is the product's identity |
| OFFER-05 | Program contents | Modules or phases with titles and one-line outcomes; number of lessons, hours, live sessions | section_audit `any_table`; manual | 7 of 14 sites list a curriculum (measured); counts make the offer concrete |
| OFFER-06 | Format and workload | Duration, hours per week, live versus recorded, access length, community, support channels and response times | manual | Answers "will this fit my week"; 12 of 14 sites show their community (measured) |
| OFFER-07 | Results | Case studies with before, after, timeframe, screenshots or graphs | no_stats; manual | The strongest proof on the page |
| OFFER-08 | Testimonials | Video and text, named | section_audit `reviews`, `video` | Named voices beat anonymous quotes |
| OFFER-09 | The coach or team | Photo, credentials, link to the about page | section_audit `author_block` | Who delivers the promise |
| OFFER-10 | Comparison table | Versus the alternatives, 5-8 criteria, real costs of each alternative | section_audit `comparison_table` | Answers "why this and not a private coach or free content" |
| OFFER-11 | Price and payment | Price as HTML text (or "from"), payment plans, what is included; if only after a call, say so and why | section_audit `price_in_text`, `pricing_block` | A price in an image or behind JavaScript is invisible to AI crawlers (C-21) |
| OFFER-12 | Guarantee | Exact terms, only when one exists | section_audit `guarantee` | A real guarantee is a differentiator in French markets |
| OFFER-13 | Bonuses | Only real ones, each with its standalone value | manual | Inflated bonuses read as a trap |
| OFFER-14 | Process to join | 3-4 numbered steps (apply, call, onboarding) with durations; booking link; what happens on the call | section_audit `process_block`, `form` | Removes the "what happens next" doubt; 5 of 14 sites run a call or application (measured) |
| OFFER-15 | For / not for | Two lists | manual | Qualification lowers refunds |
| OFFER-16 | Objection FAQ | 10-15 real objections | section_audit `faq` | The written sales call |
| OFFER-17 | Final CTA | Promise, proof, button | section_audit `cta` | The last conversion surface |
| OFFER-18 | Length | 2000+ words, never under the competitor median | thin_content; page_benchmark.py `words` | A sales page must answer every objection in text |
| OFFER-19 | Offer schema | Course (curriculum program: name, description, provider, offers, hasCourseInstance), Service (one-to-one coaching) or Product with Offer (digital product); BreadcrumbList; Organization referenced by `@id`; schema price equals the visible price; nothing not shown on the page | schema_none, schema_fields, jsonld_invalid; manual for the match | A price, rating or session marked up but not visible is a spam policy risk |
| OFFER-20 | Shared competitor blocks | Every block half or more of the benchmarked offer pages have is present | page_benchmark.py block gaps | Same as HOME-33 |

### 1.4 About or founder page (ABOUT)

The entity anchor and, on a one-expert site, the author page every article links to. The wireframe is owned by seo-page-sections (`references/transverse-pages.md`, About page); the founder variant is in `references/block-library.md` section 3. Blocks: H1 full name + role; answer-first lead (what, since when, for whom, one proof number); facts block (years, results with sources, clients, locations, certifications, press); honest dated story; proof linked to third-party sources; real social profiles; the author's articles and a CTA to the offer; ProfilePage with mainEntity Person (same `@id` as article bylines) and `sameAs`. Consistency rule: name, one-line description, founding year and location identical on the site, the profiles and the schema.

## Phase 3. Benchmark the competitors

Pick 3-5 competitors, homepage against homepage and offer page against offer page:

1. The category term SERP: the businesses ranking organically for what buyers type ("coaching poker"), not directories, marketplaces or Wikipedia.
2. The "best {category}" query ("meilleur coaching poker", "best SEO agency Lyon"): the brands the lists and roundups name.
3. The brands AI assistants name: ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews "best {category} for {audience}" and note who is recommended (geo-visibility, geo-tracking).

Prefer the brands that appear in two or more of these sources. Then:

```
python3 skills/seo-geo-audit/scripts/page_benchmark.py --type homepage https://client.com/ https://rival1.com/ https://rival2.com/ https://rival3.com/
python3 skills/seo-geo-audit/scripts/page_benchmark.py --type pricing https://client.com/offer/ https://rival1.com/programme/ https://rival2.com/coaching/ https://rival3.com/formation/
```

What to read in the output:

- The metric table: words, bold, H2, question headings, tables, statistics, placement, money links, social profiles, videos, schema types per site.
- "BLOCKS half or more of the competitors have and the client does not": the minimum for HOME-33 and OFFER-20, not the ceiling.
- "SCHEMA TYPES competitors use and the client does not": feeds HOME-31 and OFFER-19.
- "CLIENT BELOW THE COMPETITOR MEDIAN": the C and HOME rows behind those metrics move up in priority; the median word count sets HOME-19 and OFFER-18.
- What the script cannot measure, read by hand on each competitor: proof formats (numbers, video, screenshots, ratings), pricing visibility, lead magnet, free tools or resources, community, named method, guarantee. `references/block-library.md` section 5 gives how often each appeared on 14 coaching and training sites, to judge what is table stakes and what is rare.

Information gain: from the blocks few or none of the competitors ship, choose 1-3 the client can back with real facts and write them down. Typical candidates: original numbers (named student results, a results graph), a comparison table of the real costs of each alternative, a named method, a free tool, a money-back guarantee in a French market, a coach linked to a checkable third-party profile, a dated rating with its count. The conversion blocks in `references/block-library.md` section 4 list when each is worth adding. Why: the SERP shows which page shape Google already rewards for this query, and the competitors' strongest proof is the bar the client's proof must clear.

## Phase 4. Build

### 4.1 Block order

Read `references/block-library.md` now: per block minimum spec, the typical objection set, the offer sequence and the about page. Keep every block the benchmark shows on 3+ competitors, then add the information gain chosen in Phase 3.

Default homepage order:

1. Header with the primary CTA button (HOME-01)
2. Hero: H1 = category + outcome, sub-line with one proof number, primary and secondary CTA, proof strip: rating, client count, logos or press (HOME-02, HOME-03)
3. Problem block in the buyer's words (HOME-04)
4. The method, named, in 3-5 pillars (HOME-05)
5. Offer routing: one card per offer or service, each linked to its page (HOME-06)
6. Results with numbers (before, after, timeframe), case study links (HOME-07)
7. Testimonials: video plus text, real first name, photo, situation (HOME-08)
8. Founder block: photo, credentials with numbers, link to the about page, social profiles (HOME-09)
9. Comparison table: this offer versus the usual alternatives (HOME-10)
10. Who it is for / not for (HOME-11)
11. Guarantee or risk reversal, when there is one (HOME-12)
12. Lead magnet or free resource (HOME-13)
13. Latest articles: 3 cards with cover images (HOME-14)
14. FAQ: 8-12 real objections, not definitions (HOME-15)
15. Descriptive block of 300+ words on the category term (HOME-16)
16. Final CTA, then a footer with real social profiles, contact, legal links (HOME-17, HOME-18)

The offer page follows OFFER-01 to OFFER-17 in order: outcome, proof, problem, method, program contents, format and workload, results, testimonials, coach, comparison, price and payment, guarantee, bonuses, process to join, for / not for, FAQ, final CTA.

### 4.2 Copy rules

Field heuristics from 115+ agency audits unless marked measured.

| Rule | Detail | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| Length | Homepage 1500-1800 words of real text, offer page 2000+; never under the competitor median | HOME-19, OFFER-18 |
| H1 | Category term + outcome, not a slogan ("Coaching poker pour devenir rentable en cash game", not "Libère ton potentiel") | HOME-02, C-03 |
| Placement | Category term in title, H1, first 100 words, at least two H2s, meta description, one image alt, and in bold once in the intro; target 60%+ coverage of the placement matrix | HOME-20, C-10 |
| Semantic bolding | Category term bolded once in the first paragraph; one bold phrase every 100-150 words on a benefit, a number or a key fact; never whole sentences, never decoration | HOME-21, C-09 |
| Numbers | Every block carries at least one figure | HOME-22 |
| Proof attribution | Each number says where it comes from (client name, platform, date, screenshot) | HOME-23, C-14 |
| Density | No term above 2.5% of the text, brand names aside | C-11 |
| Question H2s | At least 3 H2s phrased as the questions buyers ask ("Combien de temps faut-il pour devenir rentable ?"); they map to People Also Ask and to AI sub-queries | C-13 |
| Chunk autonomy | Each block understandable alone, 40-80 word paragraphs; AI engines lift isolated passages | C-12 |
| Internal links | Every offer page, the about page and 2-3 pillar articles linked from the body | HOME-30, C-16 |
| Punctuation | Zero em dashes and en dashes, the most recognizable AI-writing tell | C-25 |
| Price | Price, range or "from", instalments or annual option; if only after a call, say so and why | HOME-24, OFFER-11 |
| Ratings | Rating + platform + count + date read | HOME-25 |
| Checkable credentials | Third-party profiles linked from the founder block and listed in `sameAs` | HOME-26 |
| Superlatives | "n°1", "le meilleur", "leader" only with a cited source next to them, otherwise remove | HOME-28 |
| Urgency | Countdowns and limited places only when the deadline or the limit is real | HOME-28 |

### 4.3 Metadata patterns

```
Homepage title:    {what the business does} for {audience} | {brand}     (50-60 chars)
Homepage meta:     {outcome}. {proof number}. {call to action}.          (120-160 chars)
Homepage H1:       {category term} + {outcome}
Offer page title:  {offer name}, {outcome} | {brand}                     (50-60 chars)
Offer page H1:     {offer name} + {outcome}
About H1:          {full name}, {role} ({brand})
```

### 4.4 Structured data

Homepage: Organization (name, url, logo, `sameAs` to every real profile, founder) plus WebSite, and the founder as a Person with `sameAs` (HOME-31). Offer page: Course when it is a program with a curriculum, Service when it is one-to-one coaching, Product with Offer for a digital product; BreadcrumbList on both offer and about pages (OFFER-19, C-19). About page: ProfilePage with the Person. Templates and field rules: seo-schema-markup (Course template in its jsonld-templates reference). Mark up only what the page shows: a price, a rating or a session that is not visible on the page is a spam policy risk.

### 4.5 Acceptance test

Re-run the Phase 2 commands and the Phase 3 benchmark on the live or staged pages. The finish line:

- No high or critical finding.
- BOLD count above zero with the category term among the samples.
- PLACEMENT coverage at 60% or more.
- No `schema_none`, `schema_entity` or `schema_fields`.
- SOCIAL shows real profiles and no placeholders.
- `home_no_money_links` absent.
- Word count at or above the competitor median.
- `section_audit.py` block list compared with the benchmark: every block present on 3+ competitors is now present (page_benchmark.py block gap list empty).
- Every C, HOME, OFFER and ABOUT row is Pass, or a remaining Fail is a written owner decision.

## Regulated and money topics (HOME-28, HOME-29)

Gambling, trading, income, health, weight loss: state results as observed outcomes of named clients, never as a guarantee; add an income or results disclaimer next to the claim and in the footer ("aucun résultat individuel n'est garanti"); for gambling, add the age limit (18+), a responsible-gambling mention and the national helpline in the site-wide footer (in France: Joueurs Info Service, 09 74 75 13 13), plus a responsible-play page. The audit flags `claim_no_disclaimer` and `gambling_notice_missing`. Editorial transparency pages (editorial policy, methodology, how results are measured) linked from the footer strengthen the same trust layer. Google rates these topics "Your Money or Your Life": trust signals are judged more strictly, and advertising rules (ARPP in France, FTC endorsement guides in the US) apply to testimonials with results.

## What the coaching benchmark taught (HOME-24 to HOME-31)

The full table is in `references/block-library.md` section 5 (14 poker coaching and training sites, October 2026, measured). What it changes for any coaching or training site: a JSON-LD graph, a visible price, a community and a lead magnet are table stakes (11-12 of 14); numbers-based proof, a coach linked to checkable third-party records, a dated rating with its count and a money-back guarantee are rare (3-5 of 14) and are the cheapest information gain.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Generic FAQ of definitions ("What is poker?") | FAQ of the real objections: price, time, level required, difference from free content, results timeline, refund | HOME-15, OFFER-16 |
| Slogan H1 | Category term + outcome | HOME-02 |
| Zero bold | Category term bolded in the intro, one bold phrase every 100-150 words on facts and benefits | HOME-21 |
| Testimonials without numbers or names | First name, starting point, result, timeframe, photo or video | HOME-07, HOME-08 |
| Founder block with no credential | Years, verifiable results, profiles, press; link to the about page | HOME-09, HOME-26 |
| Footer social icons pointing to `instagram.com/` | Real profile URLs or no icon; the same URLs in `sameAs` | HOME-27 |
| No price anywhere | A price, a range or "from"; hiding it entirely sends buyers and assistants to a competitor who shows one | HOME-24, OFFER-11 |
| Offer page with no structured data | Course, Service or Product with Offer, plus the Organization reference | OFFER-19 |
| Blog invisible from the homepage | 3 latest articles with cover images | HOME-14 |
