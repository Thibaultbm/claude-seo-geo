# Homepage and offer page that rank, get cited and sell

The homepage is the strongest page of a small site: most backlinks land there, every branded search lands there, and AI assistants answering "who is {brand}" or "best {category} coach" read it first. The flagship offer page is where the money is made. On expert and coaching sites both are usually built as a stack of slogans, three icons, a video and a generic FAQ ("What is poker?"): 800 words, no bold, no numbers, no structured data. That page cannot rank on the category term, gives an AI engine nothing to quote and gives a buyer no reason to trust.

This skill fixes that with a benchmarked block list, copy rules and a measurable finish line.

## Company knowledge first (Obsidian)

If the working environment contains an Obsidian vault or any local knowledge base (a folder of .md notes, often with a .obsidian directory), read the relevant notes before writing: offer facts, real prices, real client results, founder story and credentials, positioning, competitors, social profile URLs, and the SEO action log. Every block of a homepage asserts facts about the business, so invented numbers are the main failure mode. At the end of the session, append what was published to the vault's SEO action log. Vault structure and protocols: the obsidian-brain skill.

## When to use

- A homepage or sales page is thin, slogan-only, or has only a generic FAQ.
- An audit (`seo_audit.py`) reports `no_bold`, `thin_content`, `schema_none`, `schema_entity`, `home_no_money_links`, `social_placeholder` or a low placement coverage on the homepage.
- A site is being built from scratch, or generated by an agent, and the block list must be decided first.
- The owner asks "what are my competitors doing that I am not".

Route adjacent work: service pages and city pages to seo-content-service-page; articles and the blog template to seo-content-blog; block-by-block gap audits of any other page type to seo-page-sections; JSON-LD details to seo-schema-markup; keyword choice to seo-keyword-research.

## Workflow

### Step 1. Collect the facts the copy needs

Ask for, or pull from the vault, before writing a line. Missing facts become visible placeholders `{to confirm}`, never inventions.

| Fact | Used in |
|---|---|
| Category term and its search variants (what buyers type: "coaching poker", "formation poker", "agence SEO Lyon") | Title, H1, first 100 words, H2s |
| The outcome promised, with a number and a timeframe | Hero sub-line, results block |
| Proof numbers: clients served, years, results, ratings with their platform and count | Proof strip, results, founder block |
| 3-6 client results with first name, starting point, result, timeframe; video testimonials | Results and testimonials |
| Founder credentials that can be checked (years, track record, press, certifications, public profiles) | Founder block, Person schema |
| The offer: format, contents, time commitment, price or price range, guarantee, process to join | Offer cards, sales page |
| Real social profile URLs (LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, X, TikTok, community) | Footer, founder block, `sameAs` |
| The 8-12 objections prospects actually raise (sales calls, emails, DMs, comments) | FAQ |

### Step 2. Benchmark the competitors' blocks

Search the category term and the "best {category}" query; take the 3-5 competitors that rank or that AI assistants name. For each, run the two bundled detectors on the homepage and the main offer page:

```
python3 skills/seo-page-sections/scripts/section_audit.py --type homepage https://competitor.com/
python3 skills/seo-geo-audit/scripts/seo_audit.py https://competitor.com/ /offer-page/
```

Record per competitor: word count, bold count, blocks present (the reference block library lists the elements to look for, with how often they appeared on 14 audited coaching and training sites), schema types, proof formats (numbers, video, screenshots, ratings), pricing visibility, lead magnet, free tools or resources, community. The output of this step is a table "block x competitor" plus the list of blocks present on 3+ competitors and missing on the client site: that list is the minimum, not the ceiling. Why: the SERP shows which page shape Google already rewards for this query, and the competitors' strongest proof is the bar the client's proof must clear.

### Step 3. Build the block list

Read `references/block-library.md` now: it holds the full homepage and offer-page block lists in order, with the minimum spec and the reason for each block, the about/founder page, and the conversion blocks seen on high-performing coaching, training and service sites. Keep every block the benchmark shows on 3+ competitors, then add the blocks that create information gain (original numbers, a comparison table, a named method, a free tool).

Default homepage order:

1. Header with the primary CTA button
2. Hero: H1 = category + outcome, sub-line with one proof number, primary and secondary CTA, proof strip (rating, client count, logos or press)
3. Problem block in the buyer's words
4. The method, named, in 3-5 pillars
5. Offer routing: one card per offer or service, each linked to its page
6. Results with numbers (before, after, timeframe), case study links
7. Testimonials: video plus text, real first name, photo, situation
8. Founder block: photo, credentials with numbers, link to the about page, social profiles
9. Comparison table: this offer versus the usual alternatives
10. Who it is for / not for
11. Guarantee or risk reversal, when there is one
12. Lead magnet or free resource
13. Latest articles: 3 cards with cover images
14. FAQ: 8-12 real objections, not definitions
15. Descriptive block of 300+ words on the category term
16. Final CTA, then a footer with real social profiles, contact, legal links

The offer page follows the sales sequence in the reference (outcome, program contents, format and workload, results, coach, price and payment, guarantee, bonuses, comparison, process to join, FAQ, final CTA).

### Step 4. Write the copy

Rules (field heuristics from 115+ agency audits unless marked measured):

| Rule | Detail | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Length | Homepage 1500-1800 words of real text, offer page 2000+; never under the competitor median | A page of headlines gives ranking and retrieval nothing to work with |
| H1 | Category term + outcome, not a slogan ("Coaching poker pour devenir rentable en cash game", not "Libère ton potentiel") | The H1 is the second strongest relevance signal after the title |
| Placement | Category term in title, H1, first 100 words, at least two H2s, meta description, one image alt, and in bold once in the intro | The placement matrix the audit script measures (target 60%+ coverage) |
| Semantic bolding | The category term bolded once in the first paragraph; then one bold phrase every 100-150 words on a benefit, a number or a key fact; never whole sentences, never decoration | Bold is the cheapest visible relevance and scannability signal; skimmers read the bold and the H2s only |
| Numbers | Every block carries at least one figure: results, clients, years, hours, price, timeframe | Statistics raise generative visibility (Princeton GEO study, about +32%, measured) and specific claims convert better than adjectives |
| Proof attribution | Each number says where it comes from (client name, platform, date, screenshot) | Unattributed numbers read as marketing to both buyers and quality systems |
| Density | No term above 2.5% of the text, brand names aside | Over-optimization; the audit flags it |
| Question H2s | At least 3 H2s phrased as the questions buyers ask ("Combien de temps faut-il pour devenir rentable ?") | They map to People Also Ask and to the sub-queries AI engines generate |
| Chunk autonomy | Each block understandable alone, 40-80 word paragraphs | AI engines lift isolated passages |
| Internal links | Every offer page, the about page and 2-3 pillar articles linked from the body, not only the menu | The homepage's authority flows only through body links that carry descriptive anchors |
| Punctuation | Zero em dashes and en dashes | The most recognizable AI-writing tell |
| Price | Show the price, a range or "from", with instalments or an annual option when they exist; if the price is only given after a call, say so and why | 12 of 14 audited coaching sites show a price; hiding it sends buyers and assistants to the ones that do |
| Ratings | Rating + platform + review count + the date it was read ("4.5/5 Trustpilot, 1253 avis, relevé le 9 août 2026") | A dated, counted rating is checkable; a bare star row is not |
| Checkable credentials | Link the founder to third-party profiles that confirm the claims (the public results databases of the field, press, Wikipedia or Wikidata when they exist) and put the same URLs in `sameAs` | Claims an assistant can cross-check become entity facts; claims it cannot are ignored |
| Superlatives | "n°1", "le meilleur", "leader" only with a cited source next to them, otherwise remove | The audit flags `superlative_claim`; unbacked superlatives lower trust |
| Urgency | Countdowns and limited places only when the deadline or the limit is real | Fake urgency is a deceptive commercial practice under EU consumer law |

Regulated or money promises (gambling, trading, income, health, weight loss): state results as observed outcomes of named clients, never as a guarantee; add an income or results disclaimer next to the claim and in the footer ("aucun résultat individuel n'est garanti"); for gambling, add the age limit (18+), a responsible-gambling mention and the national helpline in the site-wide footer (in France: Joueurs Info Service, 09 74 75 13 13), plus a responsible-play page. The audit flags `claim_no_disclaimer` and `gambling_notice_missing`. Editorial transparency pages (editorial policy, methodology, how results are measured) linked from the footer strengthen the same trust layer. Google rates these topics "Your Money or Your Life": trust signals are judged more strictly, and advertising rules (ARPP in France, FTC endorsement guides in the US) apply to testimonials with results.

### Step 5. Structured data

Homepage: Organization (name, url, logo, sameAs to every real profile, founder) plus WebSite, and the founder as a Person with `sameAs`. Offer page: Course when it is a program with a curriculum, Service when it is one-to-one coaching, Product with Offer for a digital product; BreadcrumbList on both. About page: ProfilePage with the Person. Templates and field rules: seo-schema-markup (Course template in its jsonld-templates reference). Mark up only what the page shows: a price, a rating or a session that is not visible on the page is a spam policy risk.

### Step 6. Verify

Run the audit script on the live or staged pages:

```
python3 skills/seo-geo-audit/scripts/seo_audit.py https://example.com/ /offer/ /about/
```

The finish line: no high or critical finding; BOLD count above zero with the category term among the samples; PLACEMENT coverage at 60% or more; no `schema_none`, `schema_entity` or `schema_fields`; SOCIAL shows real profiles and no placeholders; `home_no_money_links` absent; word count at or above the competitor median. Then run `section_audit.py --type homepage` and compare its block list with the Step 2 benchmark table: every block present on 3+ competitors must now be present.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Generic FAQ of definitions ("What is poker?") | FAQ of the real objections: price, time, level required, difference from free content, results timeline, refund |
| Slogan H1 | Category term + outcome |
| Zero bold | Category term bolded in the intro, one bold phrase every 100-150 words on facts and benefits |
| Testimonials without numbers or names | First name, starting point, result, timeframe, photo or video |
| Founder block with no credential | Years, verifiable results, profiles, press; link to the about page |
| Footer social icons pointing to `instagram.com/` | Real profile URLs or no icon; the same URLs in `sameAs` |
| No price anywhere | A price, a range or "from"; hiding it entirely sends buyers and assistants to a competitor who shows one |
| Offer page with no structured data | Course, Service or Product with Offer, plus the Organization reference |
| Blog invisible from the homepage | 3 latest articles with cover images |
| Income promises without disclaimer | Observed results of named clients plus a disclaimer; age and responsible-play mentions on gambling topics |
